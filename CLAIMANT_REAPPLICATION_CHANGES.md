# Claimant and Medical reapplication changes — 27 October 2026

Medical reapplication uses three calendar months from the most recent approval date. For example, approval on 27 October 2026 gives a default next date of 27 January 2027. Month ends are clamped: 31 January becomes 30 April. Recording a payment does not alter this date. Historical walk-ins check eligibility against their filing date, and approval checks against their effective approval date.

After three months, the patient may submit another application without a separate reapplication permission; the new application still needs review and approval. Before that date, an administrator may grant one early application with a start date, optional expiry, and required reason. A denied, expired, consumed, or undated early exception does not extend the normal three-month wait. Each adjustment records a new decision with the default, previous, and chosen dates. A stale form cannot overwrite a newer decision. Submission checks the patient's latest active application and consumes an early permission once. Approval also rechecks the waiting period, using any dated exception consumed by that application. Each new approval starts a fresh three-month wait for that patient, across applicant accounts.

Medical representatives and Burial claimants must be at least 18 on the filing date. Medical patients under 18 must use an adult representative. OPD patients under 18 require an adult guardian; adult patients may travel alone, and any named companion must be an adult. Birthdate is collected and age is calculated on the server. Checks cover online forms, final submission, walk-ins, edits, and approval.

Existing birthdates are not invented from stored ages. Existing approvals, amounts, and permission history are preserved. Dashboard → Data records lists claimant details that need verification and older permissions without start dates. An old undated permission must receive a new dated decision before it can allow an early application. Missing historical approval dates require verification or an explicit dated exception.
